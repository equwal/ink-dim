# Privacy

Ink Dim collects nothing.

The app has no INTERNET permission. It cannot send data anywhere. There are no
advertisements and no analytics.

## What the permission does

**Shizuku (moe.shizuku.manager.permission.API_V23).** It is the only permission
the app asks for. With it the app runs three shell commands on the device: it
reads the light of the panel, it writes the light of the panel, and it sets the
system brightness back.

## What the app stores

The app stores one thing on the device: whether the light is down. That is how
the next tap knows which way to go. Android deletes it when you uninstall the
app.
